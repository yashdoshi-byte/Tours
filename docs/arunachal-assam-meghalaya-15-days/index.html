A grand circuit of India's least-crowded corner. You climb from the Brahmaputra plains through the orchid forests of Bhalukpong and the apple orchards of Dirang to Tawang, home of India's largest monastery, then come down to Kaziranga for rhinos and elephants, and finish in Meghalaya's waterfalls, caves and living root bridges. The route climbs gradually, with two nights in Dirang before Tawang, so your body has time to adjust to the altitude.

Day by day, in short

Daily travel brief

Distances are by road for the whole day. The full plan with timings comes with your detailed itinerary.

  1. 1

    Land in Guwahati, gateway to the North East

    • Private car with driver
    • About 25 km, 45 minutes
    • Optional: Brahmaputra sunset cruise: cruises stop when the river is high, mostly in the monsoon.
    • Your Arunachal Inner Line Permit is ready before you land; keep the PDF on your phone and a printout in the car.
  2. 2

    Up the Brahmaputra to the foothills of Bhalukpong

    • Private car with driver
    • About 230 km, 5 to 6 hours
    • Gangetic river dolphin boat ride: tickets are sold by the forest department one day ahead and depend on the water level.
    • The ILP is checked at Bhalukpong.
  3. 3

    Orchids of Tipi and the climb to Dirang

    • Private car with driver
    • About 160 km, 6 to 7 hours
    • Through Bomdila to Dirang: mountain roads in Arunachal can close for a few hours after landslides.
    • Drink plenty of water from today.
  4. 4

    Dirang valley, Mandala Top and Sangti

    • Private car with driver

    Dirang Dzong

    • Dirang Dzong and Dirang Monastery: cover shoulders and walk clockwise around stupas and prayer wheels.
    • This easy day is deliberate.
  5. 5

    Over Sela Pass to Tawang

    • Private car with driver
    • About 140 km, 6 to 7 hours
    • Sela Pass and Sela Lake: snow can close the pass road in winter; the tunnel keeps the route open.
    • Pack a down jacket, gloves and a cap in the car today, whatever the season.
  6. 6

    Tawang Monastery, Urgelling and the War Memorial

    • Private car with driver
    • All within 10 km

    Urgelling Monastery

    • Tawang Monastery: cover arms and legs, remove hats inside the prayer hall and never point your feet at the altar.
    • Your Bumla permit is applied for today at the Deputy Commissioner's office with your ID, ready for tomorrow.
  7. 7

    Bumla Pass and Madhuri Lake

    • Private car with driver
    • About 37 km each way, rough road

    Sangetsar Lake

    • Local taxi to Bumla: bumla is open to Indian citizens only, with a permit, and is closed on some days for Army reasons.
    • Children under 10 and anyone with heart or breathing conditions should skip Bumla and keep to the lakes.
  8. 8

    Back over the mountains to Bomdila

    • Private car with driver
    • About 180 km, 6 to 7 hours
    • Today is the last high-altitude night.
  9. 9

    Down to the grasslands of Kaziranga

    • Private car with driver
    • About 220 km, 6 to 7 hours
    • Tomorrow's safari slots are confirmed tonight.
  10. 10

    Rhinos, elephants and swamp deer in Kaziranga

    • Private car with driver

    Bagori Range Kaziranga

    • Jeep safari, Central: safaris run only when the park is open, usually 1 November to 30 April.
    • Wear earthy colours, keep voices low and never step out of the jeep.
  11. 11

    Through Assam to the hill capital of Shillong

    • Private car with driver
    • About 200 km, 5 to 6 hours
    • Meghalaya's caves and falls are at their best after the rains, from September to November.
  12. 12

    Cherrapunji: waterfalls and caves

    • Private car with driver
    • About 55 km each way, 2 hours

    Mawsmai Cave » Seven Sisters Falls

    • Mawsmai Cave: shoes with grip; the floor is wet.
    • Seven Sisters Falls and Nohkalikai Falls: many falls thin out from January to March.
  13. 13

    Mawlynnong, the living root bridge and Dawki

    • Private car with driver
    • About 90 km to Mawlynnong, 3 hours

    Riwai Living Root Bridge

    • Riwai living root bridge: steps are slippery after rain.
    • Umngot River, Dawki: the water is clearest from November to April.
  14. 14

    Back to Guwahati and the Kamakhya temple

    • Private car with driver
    • About 100 km, 3 to 4 hours
    • Kamakhya Temple: queues are long on Tuesdays, Saturdays and festival days.
    • Kamakhya Temple: leather is not allowed inside.
  15. 15

    Fly home from Guwahati

    • Private car with driver
    • About 25 km, 45 minutes
    • Thank you for travelling the North East with us.

Local know-how

Travel hacks from our desk

MONEY

UPI, cards and cash

UPI works almost everywhere, even at small stalls. Keep some cash in INR 100 and 200 notes for temples, parking, tips and places where the network is weak.

WEAR

Temple and shrine etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees. Remove shoes where asked, and carry socks for hot stone floors. Leather belts and bags are not allowed inside some Jain and Hindu temples. Ask before photographing people and inside shrines.

CARE

Water and street food

Drink sealed bottled or filtered water only. Choose busy stalls where food is cooked in front of you, and skip cut fruit and ice from roadside stalls.

TIP

Driving times

Indian roads are slower than the kilometres suggest: plan on 40 to 50 km an hour on highways and much less in hills and towns. Your driver's breaks and meal stops are built into the timings.

TIP

Plugs and tipping

Plugs are Indian three-pin and two-pin, 230 volts. Tipping drivers and guides about INR 300 to 500 a day is appreciated.

When to go

Best time to visit for this journey

October to April. October to December gives the clearest mountain views; Kaziranga opens in November; March and April bring rhododendrons and orchids. Avoid June to September, when monsoon landslides close the Arunachal roads and Kaziranga is shut.
