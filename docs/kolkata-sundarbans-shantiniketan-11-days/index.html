Bengal rewards slow travel. This route gives you Kolkata's colonial avenues, temples and food, a day at the ISKCON headquarters in Mayapur, two nights deep in the Sundarbans mangroves on a boat, a beach break at Mandarmani and the peace of Rabindranath Tagore's Shantiniketan. We changed the order of the original plan so you sleep in Kolkata between excursions instead of crossing the city twice in a day.

Distances are by road for the whole day. The full plan with timings comes with your detailed itinerary.

  1. 1

    Arrive in Kolkata, first look at the City of Joy

    • Private car with driver
    • About 9 km by road
    • Airport to city about 1 hour

    Mother House Kolkata

    • Half-day city tour: victoria Memorial museum is closed on Mondays.
    • Kalighat Kali Temple: leather is not allowed inside.
  2. 2

    Old Kolkata: Jain temple, Tagore's house and Marble Palace

    • Private car with driver

    Jorasanko Thakur Bari

    • Jorasanko Thakur Bari: closed on Mondays.
    • Marble Palace: entry needs a free pass from the tourist office; we arrange it.
  3. 3

    Mayapur, home of the Hare Krishna movement

    • Private car with driver
    • About 2 km by road
    • About 130 km each way, 3.5 to 4 hours
    • ISKCON Mayapur: the temple closes for darshan from about 13:00 to 16:00.
    • This is a long day.
  4. 4

    Into the Sundarbans mangroves

    • Private car with driver
    • About 100 km, 3 hours by road
    • Sunset cruise: resorts run on generators; air-conditioning usually works only from about 21:00 to 06:00.
    • Pack light for the Sundarbans; leave big bags at the Kolkata hotel.
  5. 5

    Watchtowers and creeks of the tiger reserve

    • Private car with driver
    • About 50 km by road
    • Back towards the resort: never put hands in the water or step off the boat except at watchtowers.
    • Carry binoculars, a cap and mosquito repellent.
  6. 6

    Back to Kolkata, then the beach at Mandarmani

    • Private car with driver
    • Boat 1.5 hours, road about 5 hours
    • Swim only near lifeguards; currents in the Bay of Bengal can be strong.
  7. 7

    Beach morning and back to Kolkata

    • Private car with driver
    • About 175 km by road
    • About 180 km, 4 hours

    Tagore's Shantiniketan

Local know-how

Travel hacks from our desk

MONEY

UPI, cards and cash

UPI works almost everywhere, even at small stalls. Keep some cash in INR 100 and 200 notes for temples, parking, tips and places where the network is weak.

WEAR

Temple and shrine etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees. Remove shoes where asked, and carry socks for hot stone floors. Leather belts and bags are not allowed inside some Jain and Hindu temples. Ask before photographing people and inside shrines.

CARE

Water and street food

Drink sealed bottled or filtered water only. Choose busy stalls where food is cooked in front of you, and skip cut fruit and ice from roadside stalls.

TIP

Driving times

Indian roads are slower than the kilometres suggest: plan on 40 to 50 km an hour on highways and much less in hills and towns. Your driver's breaks and meal stops are built into the timings.

TIP

Plugs and tipping

Plugs are Indian three-pin and two-pin, 230 volts. Tipping drivers and guides about INR 300 to 500 a day is appreciated.

November to February: cool, dry days for Kolkata's streets and the mangrove cruise.

October to March: cool, dry days for city walks and the best light in the Sundarbans. Durga Puja (September or October) is spectacular but crowded. Avoid April to June (very hot) and the monsoon from June to September.
