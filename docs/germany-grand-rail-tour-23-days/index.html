A slow, complete journey through Germany by train, with at least two nights in every city. Bavaria's castles from Munich, the music city of Leipzig, baroque Dresden, Berlin and the palaces of Potsdam, the port of Hamburg, Hannover's gardens, Cologne Cathedral, Frankfurt and the Rhine Valley, the castle of Heidelberg with a day in Baden-Baden and the Black Forest, and the car museums of Stuttgart. We updated the original plan: the Pergamon Museum in Berlin is closed for renovation until about 2027, so you visit the Neues Museum instead; we moved the Black Forest day to Heidelberg, which is closer; and Frankfurt now has a Rhine Valley day.

Local know-how

Travel hacks from our desk

TIP

German trains

Fast ICE trains link every city on this route. A German Rail Pass or advance tickets with seat reservations work best; we compare the two for your dates.

TIME

Sundays

Almost all shops are closed on Sundays in Germany. Plan shopping for Saturday.

MONEY

Hotel deposit

Use a credit card for the deposit or pre-authorisation at check-in, never a forex or debit card. Forex cards cannot hold funds, many hotels refuse them, and a credit-card hold is released faster.

MONEY

Cards and cash

On card machines, always choose to pay in euros, never in INR. Cards work almost everywhere. Carry about EUR 50 in cash for small cafés, toilets and markets.

CARE

Pickpockets

Busy stations, trams and squares are pickpocket hotspots. Keep passports in the hotel safe, carry a copy, and wear your bag in front in crowds.

TIP

Plugs, water and meal times

Plugs are the two round-pin type C and F at 230 volts (Switzerland uses type J; a type C plug fits). Tap water is safe to drink. Lunch is about 12:00 to 14:00 and dinner from about 18:30.
