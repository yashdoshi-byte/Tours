Arunachal, Assam & Meghalaya: 15 Days from Tawang to the Living Root Bridges

Monasteries above the clouds, one-horned rhinos and the wettest hills on earth

A grand circuit of India's least-crowded corner. You climb from the Brahmaputra plains through the orchid forests of Bhalukpong and the apple orchards of Dirang to Tawang, home of India's largest monastery, then come down to Kaziranga for rhinos and elephants, and finish in Meghalaya's waterfalls, caves and living root bridges. The route climbs gradually, with two nights in Dirang before Tawang, so your body has time to adjust to the altitude.

Trip highlights

  • Tawang Monastery, the largest in India, founded in 1680-81
  • Sela Pass and Sela Lake at about 4,170 m, and the Jaswantgarh war memorial
  • Bumla Pass on the India-China border and Madhuri (Sangetsar) Lake, with Army permits arranged
  • Two jeep safaris in Kaziranga, home to most of the world's one-horned rhinos
  • Nohkalikai Falls, Mawsmai Cave and the Seven Sisters Falls at Cherrapunji
  • Mawlynnong village, the living root bridge at Riwai and the clear Umngot River at Dawki

Good to know

MONEY

UPI, cards and cash

UPI works almost everywhere, even at small stalls. Keep some cash in INR 100 and 200 notes for temples, parking, tips and places where the network is weak.

WEAR

Temple and shrine etiquette

Cover shoulders and knees. Remove shoes where asked, and carry socks for hot stone floors. Leather belts and bags are not allowed inside some Jain and Hindu temples. Ask before photographing people and inside shrines.

CARE

Water and street food

Drink sealed bottled or filtered water only. Choose busy stalls where food is cooked in front of you, and skip cut fruit and ice from roadside stalls.

TIP

Driving times

Indian roads are slower than the kilometres suggest: plan on 40 to 50 km an hour on highways and much less in hills and towns. Your driver's breaks and meal stops are built into the timings.

TIP

Plugs and tipping

Plugs are Indian three-pin and two-pin, 230 volts. Tipping drivers and guides about INR 300 to 500 a day is appreciated.

Best time to go

October to April. October to December gives the clearest mountain views; Kaziranga opens in November; March and April bring rhododendrons and orchids. Avoid June to September, when monsoon landslides close the Arunachal roads and Kaziranga is shut.

Frequently asked questions

How high does this tour go, and is altitude a problem?

Tawang is at about 3,000 m, Sela Pass about 4,170 m and Bumla about 4,600 m. Two nights in Dirang first help you adjust. Walk slowly, drink water, and tell us about any heart, lung or blood-pressure condition before you book.

Can foreign nationals do this tour?

Yes, with a Protected Area Permit for Arunachal, which we arrange. Bumla Pass is open only to Indian citizens, so foreign guests visit the lakes instead.

When is Kaziranga open?

Usually from 1 November to 30 April. Outside those months we replace the safaris with tea-garden and village visits.

Can the tour be shortened?

Yes. A 10-night version skips Bhalukpong and one Shillong night, or you can do Arunachal and Kaziranga only in 9 nights.
