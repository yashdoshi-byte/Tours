A Ramayana pilgrimage through the places that Sri Lankan and Tamil tradition links with the epic. Munneswaram and Manavari, where Rama is said to have worshipped Shiva after the war; Koneswaram and the Shakti Peetham at Trincomalee; the Temple of the Tooth in Kandy; the Hanuman temple at Ramboda; Seetha Amman temple at Ashok Vatika, where Sita is believed to have been held; Divurumpola, the site of her Agni Pariksha; and the Panchamukha Anjaneyar temple in Colombo. The first two days have 4 to 6 hours on the road, and Nuwara Eliya to Colombo is about 5 hours. Sigiriya's rock fortress can be climbed early on day 3 as an option.

Day by day, in short

Daily travel brief

Distances are by road for the whole day. The full plan with timings comes with your detailed itinerary.

  1. 1

    Munneswaram and Manavari on the way to Sigiriya

    • Private car with driver-guide
    • About 5 hours of driving with stops

    Munneswaram Temple » Manavari Temple

    • Munneswaram temple near Chilaw: shoes off; men may be asked to remove shirts in the inner shrine.
    • A long arrival day.
  2. 2

    Day trip to Trincomalee

    • Private car with driver-guide
    • About 2 hours 30 minutes each way

    Koneswaram Temple

    • Koneswaram temple and Shankari Devi shrine: the temple closes in the early afternoon; arrive by about 11:00.
    • Trincomalee is hot: carry water and a hat.
  3. 3

    Sigiriya, Matale and Kandy

    • Private car with driver-guide
    • About 2 hours 30 minutes of driving

    Sri Muthumariamman Temple Matale

    • Sigiriya rock fortress: hornets nest on the rock: keep quiet and do not wear strong perfume.
    • Sigiriya rock fortress: climb before 09:00 to avoid the heat.
    • Spice garden: ayurvedic products are sold at high prices; buy only if you want to.
  4. 4

    Temple of the Tooth, then Ramboda to Nuwara Eliya

    • Private car with driver-guide
    • About 2 hours 30 minutes of driving

    Sri Baktha Hanuman Temple » Ramboda Falls

    • Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic: white or light clothing, shoulders and knees covered.
    • Sri Bhakta Hanuman temple, Ramboda: open about 07:00 to 12:00 and 15:30 to 18:30.
  5. 5

    Ashok Vatika, Divurumpola and back to Colombo

    • Private car with driver-guide
    • About 5 hours of driving

    Seetha Amman Temple » Divurumpola Temple

    • A long road day down from the hills; your driver stops for tea.
  6. 6

    Fly home from Colombo

    • Private car with driver-guide
    • About 1 hour
    • Be at the airport 3 hours before an international flight.
    • Ceylon tea makes a good gift; buy sealed packs.

Local know-how

Travel hacks from our desk

MONEY

Hotel deposit

Use a credit card for the deposit or pre-authorisation at check-in, never a forex or debit card. Forex cards cannot hold funds, many hotels refuse them, and a credit-card hold is released faster.

MONEY

Rupees

Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). Cards work in hotels and big shops; carry cash for temples, tuk-tuks and roadside stalls. Indian rupees are not accepted.

CARE

Temples

Remove shoes and hats, cover shoulders and knees, and never pose for a photo with your back to a Buddha statue. White clothing is preferred at the Temple of the Tooth. Some Hindu kovils ask men to remove shirts.

WEAR

Hill country

Nuwara Eliya is about 15°C and can drop to 5°C at night. Pack a warm layer and a rain jacket.
